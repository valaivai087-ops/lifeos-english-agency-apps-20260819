【4層遞進框架】
第1層表面理解
目標：準確描述可見元素
詞彙：50+基礎動作詞、40+基礎形容詞
句型：簡單句、直接描述
範例：完整的詳細描述所有視覺元素
第2層連貫表達 [中級關鍵]
目標：邏輯清晰地連接多個想法
詞彙：增加觀看類、細緻形容詞
句型：複合句、並列結構、轉折句
特徵：段落流暢，邏輯明確
第3層詞彙精準 [高級標誌]
目標：用精確詞彙和修辭手法表達
詞彙：高階詞彙、交流類動詞
句型：複雜句、修辭句型
特徵：詞彙搭配自然，修辭優雅
第4層批判反思 [滿分必備]
目標：探討深層意義和人生價值
詞彙：概念詞、反思詞
句型：哲學性句型、引發思考
特徵：思辨深度、人生價值、批判視角
【範例1】初級 - 第1層+第2層
In this park, happy children play together. A boy runs freely on the grass while another child sits peacefully on a bench. The bright sunlight makes everything look beautiful.

The park is clean and green. Children enjoy outdoor activities. We can see their happiness and enjoy quality time together. This simple moment teaches us the value of nature and friendship.
【範例2】中級 - 第2層+第3層
This office scene brilliantly demonstrates professional teamwork. Three dedicated professionals sit in a meeting, deeply engaged in meaningful discussion. The man presents ideas confidently while the women listen actively and take notes.

What's striking is their mutual respect and genuine engagement. Not only do they work hard, but they also support one another. The organized environment reflects their positive attitude. Such cooperation shows why successful teams achieve more together than alone.
【範例3】高級 - 第3層+第4層
This powerful image captures the poignant reality of modern urban life. An elderly man sits silently on a street corner, observing the world with quiet dignity. Around him, countless pedestrians rush past, each absorbed in personal pursuits. The stark contrast between his stillness and their frenetic movement speaks volumes about contemporary society.

Yet within this seeming isolation lies profound wisdom. His patient gaze suggests both acceptance and perhaps subtle questioning. This moment crystallizes an essential human dilemma: in our endless pursuit of success, have we lost sight of presence? The image powerfully reminds us that true connection often requires slowing down.

□ 表達優雅自然
第4層檢查 (5項):
□ 超越表面描述
□ 包含批判思考
□ 討論人生價值
□ 結尾有力深刻
□ 全文展現思辨
【誤區 #1-10】詞彙和表達
❌ 誤區#1：重複詞彙
例：walk walk walking walked
✅ 改：walk / move / stroll / travel
❌ 誤區#2：相同句式
例：He is. She is. It is.
✅ 改：變化主語、動詞、句型
❌ 誤區#3：超出GSAT範圍
例：perspicacious / perambulate
✅ 改：smart / walk (GSAT範圍)
❌ 誤區#4：缺乏邏輯連接
例：A. B. C. (都是獨立句)
✅ 改：While A, B happens. However, C...
❌ 誤區#5：動詞選擇不當
例：She goes to emotion. He does happy.
✅ 改：She expresses emotion. He radiates happiness.
❌ 誤區#6：過度使用"There is"
例：There is a man. There is a woman.
✅ 改：A man and woman sit together.
❌ 誤區#7：形容詞位置錯誤
例：A bright and clean office appears
✅ 改：The bright, clean office appears
❌ 誤區#8：過度重複代詞
例：He walks. He eats. He talks. He plays.
✅ 改：He walks, eats, and talks.
❌ 誤區#9：描述不具體
例：Person does something good.
✅ 改：Teacher guides student patiently.
❌ 誤區#10：詞彙搭配不當
例：strongly happy / deeply big
✅ 改：genuinely happy / very big
【誤區 #11-20】結構和組織
❌ 誤區#11：結尾太淺
例：This is nice. I like it.
✅ 改：This teaches us about human values.
❌ 誤區#12：忽略細節
例：People work. They busy.
✅ 改：描述表情、姿態、環境
❌ 誤區#13：開頭平凡
例：There is a picture about people.
✅ 改：This powerful image captures...
❌ 誤區#14：焦點不明
例：有樹、有人、有鳥、有天空
✅ 改：清楚選擇主要焦點
❌ 誤區#15：段落太長
例：整個作文一個超長段落
✅ 改：分成開/中/結 3-4段
❌ 誤區#16：缺乏轉接詞
例：全是獨立陳述句
✅ 改：Therefore, Moreover, As a result
❌ 誤區#17：忽視對比
例：年輕人開心。老人也開心。
✅ 改：While young express joy actively...
❌ 誤區#18：過度推測
例：她一定在想過去的悲事
✅ 改：她的表情暗示深刻思考
❌ 誤區#19：邏輯跳躍
例：句子間沒有明確因果關係
✅ 改：建立清楚的邏輯流向
❌ 誤區#20：內容牽強
例：為達字數加入無關內容
✅ 改：深化已有內容而不是添加
【誤區 #21-30】文法和細節
❌ 誤區#21：時態混亂
例：walks / is eating / walked
✅ 改：保持統一時態
❌ 誤區#22：主謂不一致
例：The group are working
✅ 改：The group is working
❌ 誤區#23：拼寫錯誤
例：occured / recieve / thier
✅ 改：occurred / receive / their
❌ 誤區#24：標點混亂
例：缺少句號；逗號濫用
✅ 改：檢查每句結尾和標點
❌ 誤區#25：冠詞誤用
例：She is teacher (缺a)
✅ 改：She is a teacher
❌ 誤區#26：句子太長
例：超過30個詞的複雜句
✅ 改：分句或使用短句
❌ 誤區#27：習慣用語混亂
例：强烈混用中文表達邏輯
✅ 改：用英文的自然邏輯表達
❌ 誤區#28：音調不一致
例：super cool / awesome / mega
✅ 改：保持正式、學術音調
❌ 誤區#29：動詞時態運用
例：混用現在簡單式和進行式
✅ 改：根據語境選擇合適時態
❌ 誤區#30：介詞使用錯誤
例：in the park / at street / on situation
✅ 改：in the park / on the street
